Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Naughty or Heist is a party game that runs entirely on the phone it's installed on. This page explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't connect to the internet. We, the developer, never receive any information about you or your game nights.

What stays on your phone

To make the game work, the app keeps a few things in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your current game night, including the player names you type in, so you can resume if the app closes. A saved night is cleared after 12 hours or when you start a new one.
  • Which missions your group has already played, so the game can show fresh ones next time. This is a list of mission names only.
  • The last crew's player names and room names, so the same group can start the next night in one tap. Starting a night with a different crew replaces them.

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Camera

The camera is used only in one mission, The Lineup, and only after you allow it. Photos are taken inside the app, kept in its private storage for that round, and deleted as soon as the round ends, when you leave the mission, and every time the app starts. Photos are never saved to your photo library and are never sent anywhere. The app doesn't use the microphone.

Children

Naughty or Heist is for adults only. Everyone playing must be 18 or older, and the app asks the group to confirm this before every game. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
