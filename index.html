NaughtyorHEIST

A heist party game with dares and clothing challenges.

18+ · 2 to 8 players · One phone

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

Plan the job. Pull it off.

Your friends become a crew of thieves at a glamorous 1920s masquerade. Slip past the guards, crack the vault, fool the inspector and make the getaway with the bigger haul.

One phone runs the whole night. Pass it around, follow the story and play every mission together in the room. No accounts, no internet connection needed, no ads.

Two ways to play

4 to 8 players

The Heist

Two rival crews go head to head across seven stops and five chapters of puzzles, bluffing, quick hands and cheeky dares.

2 or 3 players

The Inside Job

A closer heist for two or three. Build the haul together, then make one last choice in secret: trust your partners, or double cross them.

You set the heat

  • Everyone privately picks Shallow, Medium or Deep. The room plays at the lowest pick, and nobody sees who chose what.
  • Anyone can lower the level at any moment with one tap.
  • Every card can be passed or swapped, free and unannounced.
  • Say "pause" and the clock stops.

The one rule above all: show only what you'd be comfortable showing at a party with friends, and what you'll still feel fine about tomorrow.

For groups where everyone is 18 or older.
